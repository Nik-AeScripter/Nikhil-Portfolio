— About

A creator who
lives in the cut.

Hey, I'm Nikhil Dere (you can call me Nick) — a Motion Designer and Video Editor from Mumbai. My creative journey started with a deep curiosity for how visuals and sound come together to tell powerful stories.

What began as simple edits and motion experiments turned into a career built on bringing ideas to life through design, animation, and rhythm. Over the past five years, I've worked on brand videos, product explainers, promotional campaigns, and social media content. My background in Multimedia and Communication (BAMMC) from Reena Mehta College, along with hands-on experience at ZI Systech Pvt. Ltd. and GetSetFly Media, has shaped my skills in storytelling, visual design, and editing with precision and intent.

Today, my goal is simple — to create visuals that move people, both emotionally and visually. Whether it's smooth transitions, dynamic typography, or perfectly timed cuts, I believe motion design isn't just about movement — it's about emotion, clarity, and making every frame count.
